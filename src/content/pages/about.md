We cook Mediterranean food for the way people actually eat together in Tampa Bay: a labeled lunch for each person at a team meeting, a family table on a busy weeknight, and buffet trays for a shower, a birthday or a wedding.

Our menu is built around a few things done carefully. Chicken shawarma roasted and sliced thin. Hummus and baba ghanoush made with tahini and lemon. Herb salads that hold up on a buffet. Warm pita packed so it is still soft when it arrives.

We try to make ordering as clear as the food. You can see what each package includes, roughly how many people it feeds, the starting price and how much notice we need. When an order needs a closer look, such as a custom menu or a large event, we send you an itemized quote before you pay anything.
