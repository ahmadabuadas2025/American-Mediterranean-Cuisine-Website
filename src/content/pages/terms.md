## Requests and confirmed orders

Submitting the order builder or a contact form sends us a request. A request is not a confirmed order. Your order is confirmed only when we confirm availability and, where applicable, payment has been completed.

## Prices

Prices shown on this website are starting prices. Delivery, setup, staffing, rentals, rush fees and tax are shown or quoted before you pay. We never describe an order as all-inclusive unless every one of those items is listed.

## Lead times and rush orders

Standard orders need advance notice. Orders inside our standard lead time may be accepted as rush orders with a disclosed rush fee, depending on kitchen capacity.

## Events and deposits

Events may require a deposit to hold the date. Deposit, balance and final-count terms are stated in your quote.

## Changes and cancellations

The cancellation, change and refund schedule is being finalized and will be published here before launch.

## Dietary information

Dietary markers are provided in good faith based on current recipes. Our kitchen handles common allergens and cross-contact is possible. Tell us about allergies before ordering.

## Servings

Serving counts are estimates and depend on the menu mix and appetite.
