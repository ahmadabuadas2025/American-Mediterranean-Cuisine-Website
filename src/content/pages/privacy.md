## What we collect

When you send an order request, event inquiry, contact message or newsletter sign-up, we collect the details you enter: your name, email, phone number, delivery address, event date, guest count, menu selections, dietary or allergen notes and any instructions.

## How we use it

We use these details to prepare quotes, confirm and deliver orders, answer questions and, if you opt in, send occasional updates. Dietary and allergen notes are used only to prepare your food.

## Payments

If online payment is enabled, payment is handled by our payment provider on their secure checkout page. We do not see or store your full card number.

## Analytics

With your consent, we use privacy-focused analytics to understand which pages are used. We do not send your name, contact details or dietary notes to analytics providers. You can change your choice at any time with the cookie settings link in the footer.

## Service providers

We use hosting, form handling, email delivery and (if enabled) payment providers to run this website. They process data on our behalf.

## Retention and your choices

Retention periods are being finalized by the owner. You can ask us to access, correct or delete your information by contacting us.
